# Primary User Groups

**Status:** Draft
**Milestone:** 1 — Product Vision & Platform Strategy
**Date:** 2026-08-06

Minara-LMS serves seven primary user groups, each corresponding to a
portal. This document describes each group's role and core needs at the
product level — not their specific permissions or UI, which belong to
later, more technical milestones.

## 1. Students
The learner population across all Minara schools and Prepped programs.
Needs: access courses, complete lessons, take assessments, view grades
and progress, manage payments, communicate with faculty/staff, receive
AI-assisted learning support, and (eventually) receive certificates.

## 2. Faculty
Instructors responsible for course delivery. Needs: manage course
content and sections, monitor student progress, grade work, give
feedback, communicate with students, and view course-level analytics.

## 3. Program Directors
Own the health and integrity of a program as a whole, above the level of
an individual course. Needs: curriculum oversight, cohort management,
faculty coordination, and program-level reporting.

## 4. Admissions Staff
Manage the prospective-to-enrolled student pipeline. Needs: process
applications, guide student onboarding, manage enrollment workflow, and
track application/enrollment status.

## 5. Clinical/Externship Coordinators
Manage the real-world, off-campus component of health sciences training.
Needs: manage externship sites, place students, track clinical hours,
and record evaluations. This group's work carries direct licensure and
accreditation weight.

## 6. Employer Partners
External organizations that engage with Minara's talent pipeline. Needs:
manage the partnership relationship, evaluate students (e.g., during or
after externship/placement), and participate in a hiring pipeline.

## 7. Institutional Administrators
Own institution-wide operational concerns that cut across programs and
schools. Needs: institution-wide management, cross-program reporting,
permissions administration, and finance oversight.

## Cross-Cutting Notes

- **One account, one profile, multiple programs.** A single person may
  hold more than one relationship to the platform over time (e.g., a
  graduate who becomes an employer contact, or a student enrolled in more
  than one program). The user-group model describes *roles a person can
  hold*, not mutually exclusive account types.
- **AI Learning Assistant is a platform capability, not a user group.**
  It acts on behalf of and in support of the groups above, primarily
  Students, and is not modeled as an independent actor with its own
  portal.

## ⚠️ Needs Verification
- Whether additional user groups (e.g., a dedicated Finance/Billing role
  distinct from Admin, or a Compliance/Accreditation-liaison role) will
  be needed is not yet confirmed and should be revisited as
  Minara-Master-Plan's governance structure becomes concrete.
- Whether a person's multiple roles (e.g., faculty who is also a program
  director) require simultaneous multi-role access in one session is not
  yet confirmed; assumed to be a natural extension of RBAC but not yet
  validated with stakeholders.
