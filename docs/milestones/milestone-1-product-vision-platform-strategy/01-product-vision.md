# Product Vision

**Status:** Draft
**Milestone:** 1 — Product Vision & Platform Strategy
**Date:** 2026-08-06

## Statement

Minara-LMS exists to be the single software platform through which the
Minara Institute of Health Sciences ecosystem delivers, manages, and
continuously improves the educational experience for every learner it
serves — across every school, every program, and every stage of a
student's journey from application to certification and beyond.

## What This Vision Means

**One platform, many programs.** A student, faculty member, or
administrator should never need to think of Minara's various schools and
programs as separate systems. Minara-LMS is the connective infrastructure
underneath all of them.

**The educational experience, not just the content.** Minara-Curriculum
defines *what* is taught. Minara-LMS defines *how it is delivered,
tracked, supported, and improved* — the lived experience of teaching and
learning, including progress, assessment, feedback, communication, and
support.

**Built to grow, not to be rebuilt.** The vision is a platform whose
architecture anticipates growth in schools, programs, and student
populations from the outset, rather than one that must be re-architected
each time the institution expands.

**Human learning, AI-assisted.** Minara-LMS envisions a platform where
artificial intelligence meaningfully supports learners and educators —
always as an assistant to human judgment and human-reviewed workflows,
never as an unsupervised decision-maker in matters of academic or
clinical consequence.

**A platform, not a silo.** Minara-LMS is designed to power not only
Minara's own schools and programs, but also the broader "Prepped" family
of learning experiences (PharmTechPrepped, PharmDPrepped, MedCodePrepped,
TherapyPrepped) as products built *on* the platform rather than as
independent systems beside it.

## Why This Vision Matters

Health sciences education carries obligations that generic LMS platforms
are not built around: accreditation scrutiny, clinical/externship
tracking, multi-program credentialing, and a student population that may
move between programs over time while remaining, fundamentally, one
learner with one record. A platform-first vision — one account, one
profile, many programs — is what allows Minara to scale its educational
offerings without fragmenting the learner's identity or the institution's
oversight of them.

## ⚠️ Needs Verification
