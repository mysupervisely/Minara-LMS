# Product Mission

**Status:** Draft
**Milestone:** 1 — Product Vision & Platform Strategy
**Date:** 2026-08-06

## Statement

The mission of Minara-LMS is to give every role in the Minara Institute of
Health Sciences ecosystem — students, faculty, program directors,
admissions staff, clinical coordinators, employer partners, and
institutional administrators — a reliable, role-appropriate way to
accomplish their part of the educational process, on one connected
platform, from day one of a program build-out onward.

Where the [Product Vision](./01-product-vision.md) describes the
long-term destination, the Product Mission describes the job the platform
must do now and as each capability comes online: support the operation of
real programs, for real students, with real accreditation and compliance
stakes.

## Mission in Practice

- **For students:** a single place to access courses, complete lessons,
  take assessments, track progress, view grades, manage payments,
  communicate, and get AI-assisted learning support — regardless of which
  program or school they are enrolled in.
- **For faculty:** a single place to manage courses, monitor student
  progress, grade work, give feedback, communicate with students, and see
  meaningful analytics about their sections.
- **For program directors:** oversight of curriculum delivery, cohort
  health, faculty coordination, and program-level reporting.
- **For admissions:** a structured, trackable path from application
  through onboarding to enrollment.
- **For clinical/externship coordinators:** management of externship
  sites, student placements, hours tracking, and evaluations — a
  domain with real-world compliance and licensure stakes.
- **For employer partners:** a way to engage with the institution's
  talent pipeline, evaluate students, and participate in hiring.
- **For institutional administrators:** institution-wide visibility,
  reporting, permissions control, and finance oversight.

## What the Mission Deliberately Does Not Include

The mission is scoped to *platform delivery and management* of the
educational experience. It does not include authoring academic content
(owned by Minara-Curriculum) or setting institutional policy, governance,
or accreditation strategy (owned by Minara-Master-Plan). See
[Scope Boundaries](./09-scope-boundaries.md) for the full boundary
definition.
