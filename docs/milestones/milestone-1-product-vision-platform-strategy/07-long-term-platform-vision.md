# Long-Term Platform Vision

**Status:** Draft
**Milestone:** 1 — Product Vision & Platform Strategy
**Date:** 2026-08-06

This document extends the [Product Vision](./01-product-vision.md) into a
longer time horizon: what Minara-LMS is meant to become once its
foundational capabilities are in place, not just what it must do at
launch.

## From Program Support to Institutional Infrastructure

In the long term, Minara-LMS is intended to be the operating system for
Minara's entire academic and administrative life cycle — not a tool bolted
onto individual programs, but the infrastructure through which new
schools and programs are stood up, run, evaluated, and improved.

## A Growing Family of Learning Experiences

The long-term vision includes the Prepped family (PharmTechPrepped,
PharmDPrepped, MedCodePrepped, TherapyPrepped) growing alongside Minara's
core schools, with the platform absorbing new learning experiences as
configuration and content, not as new engineering efforts. The intended
end state is that launching a new program or Prepped product is
primarily a curriculum and configuration exercise, not a software
project.

## One Learner, One Lifelong Record

Long term, the platform is envisioned as maintaining a durable learner
identity that can persist across multiple programs, multiple schools, and
multiple points of re-engagement over a person's career in health
sciences — from initial enrollment, through certification, through
possible return for additional programs, and potentially into an
employer or alumni relationship.

## AI-Assisted Learning at Scale

The long-term vision includes AI as a mature, trusted part of the
learning experience: assisting students with learning support,
potentially assisting faculty with insight into student needs, and
supporting analytics — always bounded by human-reviewed workflows for
anything with academic, disciplinary, or clinical consequence (see
[Guiding Principles](./03-guiding-principles.md)).

## Accreditation-Ready by Design

As Minara's programs pursue and maintain accreditation, the platform is
envisioned as a system that can produce the evidentiary record
(completion data, assessment records, externship hours, audit trails)
accreditation bodies require, as a natural byproduct of normal platform
operation rather than as special-purpose reporting built after the fact.

## An Ecosystem, Not Just a Product

Long term, the platform anticipates deeper engagement from employer
partners (hiring pipelines informed by verified student performance) and
potentially from other institutions or partners in the health sciences
education space, subject to the scope confirmation noted below.

## ⚠️ Needs Verification

- Whether Minara-LMS is ever intended to be offered to external
  institutions (i.e., as a platform/product beyond the Minara ecosystem)
  is **not confirmed**. This document does not assume that as a goal; it
  is flagged here because it is a natural extension of "platform, not
  silo" thinking and should be explicitly confirmed or ruled out by
  stakeholders.
- The long-term relationship between Minara-LMS and any future
  employer-facing marketplace or job-matching functionality is directional
  only; no specific employer integration model is confirmed.
